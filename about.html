About Us

FOGER DOWNLOADER is a free web tool that helps people save public videos and photos from supported websites to their own phone or computer.

What we do

You paste a link to a public post, choose a quality when it is a video, and download the file. No account is required. The site works on mobile, tablet and desktop.

Supported websites

TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), Vimeo, Dailymotion, Pinterest, and direct image links. YouTube is not supported.

Our approach to responsible use

We believe creators deserve credit and control over their work. This tool is intended only for content that you own, content in the public domain, or content you have clear permission to save. We do not support private, login-only, or DRM-protected content, and we do not try to bypass those protections.

Copyright owners can ask us to remove or block content through our DMCA page.
